description: An AI generated job interview for a VP of Engineering

### Executive Leadership and Company Strategy

##### Strategic Alignment:

- How do you translate company goals into a multi-year engineering strategy?
- How do you decide where engineering should lead versus support other functions?

##### Business Impact:

- Which engineering metrics best reflect business outcomes, and why?
- How do you connect technical investments to revenue growth, margin, or customer retention?

##### Decision Framework:

- How do you make high-stakes decisions under uncertainty with incomplete data?
- Describe a strategic decision that materially changed your organization trajectory.

### Organization Architecture

##### Org Design:

- What principles guide how you structure engineering at different company stages?
- How do you decide centralized versus embedded platform capabilities?

##### Leadership Team:

- How do you build and run an effective staff of Directors and senior engineering leaders?
- What do you look for when hiring leaders two levels below you?

##### Scaling:

- What operating signals tell you your organization model needs to evolve?
- How do you scale communication quality as the engineering organization grows?

### Operating Excellence

##### Planning and Portfolio:

- How do you run annual and quarterly planning across engineering portfolios?
- How do you evaluate whether portfolio mix is balanced across innovation, reliability, and efficiency?

##### Execution Risk:

- How do you identify execution risk early across multiple large programs?
- What mechanisms do you use to recover programs that are off track?

##### Reliability and Security:

- How do you ensure reliability and security are first-class priorities at the executive level?
- What governance model do you use for incident accountability and follow-through?

### Financial and Resource Management

##### Budget Ownership:

- How do you allocate budget across headcount, infrastructure, and vendor spend?
- What is your approach to cost optimization without sacrificing strategic momentum?

##### Headcount Strategy:

- How do you make hiring tradeoffs during growth periods versus constrained periods?
- How do you evaluate whether a hiring plan is realistic for execution goals?

##### Productivity and Efficiency:

- How do you measure engineering productivity without creating unhealthy incentives?
- What initiatives have you led that improved efficiency with measurable outcomes?

### Cross-Functional and External Leadership

##### Executive Partnership:

- How do you partner with Product, Design, Sales, and Customer Success at the executive level?
- Describe a case where executive priorities conflicted and how you aligned the team.

##### Board Communication:

- How do you communicate engineering strategy and risk to a board audience?
- What signals do you include in board materials to build trust and clarity?

##### Culture and Values:

- How do you shape leadership culture across geographically distributed teams?
- What actions do you take when leadership behavior undermines company values?

### Behavioral Questions

##### Crisis Leadership:

- Tell me about the most serious operational or organizational crisis you led through.
- What did you change structurally afterward to prevent recurrence?

##### Reflection:

- Describe a major miss in your leadership tenure and what you learned from it.
- How has your leadership model changed over the last three years?

### Closing Questions

##### Role Expectations:

- What outcomes would you target in your first two quarters in this role?
- What organizational support is required for sustainable success?

##### Candidate Questions:

- What questions do you have about company direction, executive expectations, or board dynamics?
