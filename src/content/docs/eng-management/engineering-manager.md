title: Engineering Manager
description: An AI generated job interview for an Engineering Manager
---

### People Leadership

##### Team Health:

- How do you assess and improve the health of an engineering team?
- What signals tell you burnout risk is increasing, and how do you respond?

##### Performance Management:

- How do you set clear expectations for engineers at different levels?
- Describe how you handled an ongoing underperformance situation.

##### Career Development:

- How do you build growth plans that balance business needs and individual goals?
- What is your approach to coaching engineers toward promotion readiness?

### Execution and Delivery

##### Planning:

- How do you run planning for a team with both roadmap and unplanned operational work?
- How do you decide which projects to delay when capacity is constrained?

##### Prioritization:

- How do you resolve conflicts between product urgency and technical debt work?
- Describe a time you reset priorities mid-quarter and kept delivery predictable.

##### Accountability:

- How do you track commitments without creating micromanagement?
- What do you do when a team repeatedly misses deadlines?

### Technical and Product Judgment

##### Technical Decision-Making:

- How involved should an Engineering Manager be in technical design decisions?
- How do you support high technical standards if you are not the deepest domain expert?

##### Product Partnership:

- How do you work with Product and Design to shape scope before implementation starts?
- How do you handle disagreements with Product about timelines or feasibility?

##### Reliability and Quality:

- How do you ensure reliability, security, and quality are built into delivery plans?
- What KPIs do you track to evaluate engineering effectiveness beyond velocity?

### Communication and Stakeholder Management

##### Upward Communication:

- How do you communicate risk and delivery confidence to senior leadership?
- What information belongs in weekly updates to maximize trust and clarity?

##### Cross-Team Collaboration:

- How do you manage dependencies with other teams that have different priorities?
- Describe a time you had to escalate a cross-team blocker.

##### Incident Leadership:

- What role do you play during high-severity incidents?
- How do you ensure incidents turn into durable process or system improvements?

### Behavioral Questions

##### Leadership Under Pressure:

- Tell me about a difficult leadership decision you made with incomplete information.
- Describe a conflict between two strong engineers and how you resolved it.

##### Learning and Adaptation:

- Share a management mistake you made and what changed afterward.
- How has your management style evolved over time?

### Closing Questions

##### Role Expectations:

- What do you need from your manager and peers to succeed in this role?
- How do you define a successful first 90 days as an Engineering Manager?

##### Candidate Questions:

- What questions do you have about team culture, leadership expectations, or product direction?
